Shri Krishna(The Creator of world)

Lord Krishna is a major deity in Hinduism,
revered as the eighth avatar of Vishnu and celebrated
for his teachings in the Bhagavad Gita and his role in the Mahabharata.

Birth and Early Life
Lord Krishna was born to Vasudeva and Devaki in Mathura, India.
His birth was prophesied to bring about the downfall of
his maternal uncle, Kamsa, who had imprisoned his parents.
To protect Krishna, he was smuggled across the Yamuna River to Gokula,
where he was raised by Nanda and Yashoda, the leaders of the cowherds

Attributes and Iconography
Krishna is often depicted as a young boy playingthe flute,
surrounded by gopis (cowherd girls), or as a divine hero.
His iconography reflects various aspects of his life, including
his childhood mischief,his role as a lover, and his wisdom as a charioteer
during the Kurukshetra War The name "Krishna" means "dark" or "black,"
which is often associated with his complexion.
Teachings and Philosophy
Krishna's teachings are primarily found in the Bhagavad Gita,
where he imparts spiritual wisdom to the warrior Arjuna on the battlefield.
The Gita addresses the moral and philosophical dilemmas
faced by individuals and emphasizes the importance of duty (dharma),
devotion (bhakti), and righteousness

Cultural Significance
Krishna is celebrated in various festivals,
the most notable being Krishna Janmashtami,
which marks his birth. His life stories, known as Krishna Līlā,
are depicted in numerous art forms, including dance, music, and drama,
and have inspired various cultural traditions across India and beyond
Worship and Devotion
Krishna is worshipped in many forms and names, such as Govinda, Gopala, and Mohan, reflecting his various attributes and roles.
His followers, known as Vaishnavas, consider him the Supreme God, and his teachings continue to inspire millions around the world
